Amazon Campaign Scraper

Privacy Policy | 隐私权政策

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction | 简介

English

Amazon Campaign Scraper is a browser extension designed to help authorized users extract advertising campaign names (Campaign Name) and advertising IDs (adId) from Amazon advertising management pages. It supports batch URL processing, extraction status tracking and CSV export.

中文

Amazon Campaign Scraper 是一款浏览器扩展程序， 帮助获得相应访问权限的用户从亚马逊广告管理页面 批量提取广告活动名称（Campaign Name）和 广告 ID（adId），支持批量网址处理、 抓取状态记录及 CSV 文件导出。

2. Information We Process | 处理的信息

English

The extension processes information including:

  • URLs submitted by users for batch processing.
  • Advertising campaign names and adId values.
  • Task progress, extraction status and error records.
  • Extension preferences and local task settings.
  • Email addresses and authentication information required for login and authorization.

中文

本扩展处理的信息包括：

  • 用户主动提交的批量网页地址。
  • 广告活动名称及广告 ID。
  • 抓取进度、执行状态及错误记录。
  • 插件设置及本地任务配置。
  • 用户登录及授权验证所需的邮箱和身份验证信息。

3. Data Usage | 数据使用目的

English

Information is processed solely to provide the extension's functionality, including extracting advertising information, tracking task execution, generating CSV files, maintaining user settings and verifying authorized access.

中文

相关信息用于实现插件的核心功能， 包括广告信息提取、任务进度记录、 CSV 文件生成、用户设置保存及客户授权验证。

4. Local Data Storage | 本地数据存储

English

Advertising extraction results, submitted URLs, task status and extension preferences are stored locally in the user's browser. Exported CSV files are saved to the user's device. These advertising extraction records are not intentionally uploaded to the authorization server.

中文

广告抓取结果、用户提交的网址、 任务执行状态及插件设置保存在用户浏览器本地。 导出的 CSV 文件保存在用户设备中。 广告抓取记录不会被主动上传至授权验证服务器。

5. Supabase Authentication | Supabase 登录与授权

English

The extension uses Supabase as its authentication and authorization service provider. Users sign in using an email address and password. Supabase processes authentication requests, manages authentication sessions and supports verification of access permissions.

Authentication-related information, such as email addresses, authentication credentials, session tokens and relevant technical metadata, may be processed by Supabase as necessary to provide secure authentication services.

Passwords are submitted through the authentication process and are not included in exported CSV files. Advertising campaign extraction data is separate from the authentication process.

中文

本扩展使用 Supabase 提供登录及授权验证服务。 用户通过邮箱和密码登录。 Supabase 用于处理身份验证请求、 管理登录会话以及支持用户访问权限验证。

在提供身份验证服务的过程中， Supabase 可能处理用户邮箱、身份验证凭据、 会话令牌及相关技术元数据。

用户密码通过身份验证流程提交， 不会包含在导出的 CSV 文件中。 广告抓取数据与身份验证流程相互独立。

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

6. Data Sharing | 数据共享

English

Authentication information is processed by Supabase to provide login and authorization functionality. Advertising extraction results are intended to remain on the user's device and are not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

We do not sell personal information or use collected information for behavioral advertising.

中文

登录和授权相关信息由 Supabase 处理， 用于提供身份验证及访问权限管理功能。 广告抓取结果按设计保留在用户设备本地， 不会用于出售或广告营销目的。

我们不会出售用户个人信息， 也不会将所处理的信息用于行为广告。

7. Browser Permissions | 浏览器权限

English

Depending on the extension version, browser permissions may be used for local storage, tab management, script execution, file downloads and access to user-specified advertising pages.

These permissions support batch processing, campaign information extraction, task tracking and CSV export.

中文

根据插件版本，扩展可能使用浏览器本地存储、 标签页管理、脚本执行、文件下载及目标网页访问权限。 这些权限用于实现批量处理、广告信息提取、 任务记录及 CSV 文件导出。

8. Data Security | 数据安全

English

We seek to limit information processing to what is necessary for the extension's functionality. Authentication is provided through Supabase. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

中文

我们尽量将信息处理限制在实现插件功能所必需的范围内。 身份验证通过 Supabase 提供。 任何电子存储或网络传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

9. Data Retention and Deletion | 数据保留与删除

English

Users may delete exported CSV files from their devices and remove extension-managed local data through browser settings or by uninstalling the extension.

Authentication account information may be retained in Supabase while the account remains active or as otherwise required for legitimate operational and legal purposes. Users may request account or personal data deletion by contacting the developer.

中文

用户可以自行删除设备上的 CSV 文件， 并通过浏览器设置或卸载插件清除扩展管理的本地数据。

Supabase 中的身份验证账户信息可能在账户有效期间， 或根据合理运营及法律要求保留。 用户可以联系开发者申请删除账户或相关个人数据。
